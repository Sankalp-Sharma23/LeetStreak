# ENO scene keyframes

The preview uses at least one transparent PNG key pose per second. Browser motion handles position changes and in-between movement. Dialogue text is separate from character PNGs.

- Scene 1 — 6 seconds: hidden at 0s; peek at 1s; reveal at 2s; viewer gaze at 3s; hold at 4s; exclamation at 5s.
- Scene 2 — 5 seconds: anticipation at 0s, alternating walk poses through 3s, planted stop at 4s.
- Scene 3 — 4 seconds: turn front at 0s, bow anticipation at 1s, low bow at 2s, upright greeting at 3s.
- Scene 4 — 4 seconds: neutral continuation at 0s, head tilt at 1s, speaking look at 2s, straighten at 3s.
- Scene 5 — 10 seconds: sit at 0–1s, retrieve laptop at 2–3s, type for exactly 3 seconds at 4–6s, close at 7s, stow at 8s, stand at 9s.
- Scene 6 — 4 seconds: neutral continuation at 0s, smirk at 1s, speak at 2s, smug finish at 3s.
- Scene 7 — 4 seconds: goodbye at 0s, turn at 1s, rightward walk at 2–3s, then leave the viewport.
